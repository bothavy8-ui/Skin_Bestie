Hyaluronic Acid

The ultimate ultimate hydration hero: what it is, key benefits, and application steps.

What is Hyaluronic Acid?

Hyaluronic Acid (HA) is a naturally occurring substance found throughout the human body, especially in the skin, eyes, and connective tissues. Its primary function is to attract and retain water, helping keep the skin hydrated, soft, and healthy.

One gram of Hyaluronic Acid can hold up to 1,000 times its weight in water, making it one of the most effective hydrating ingredients in skincare. As we age, the natural amount of Hyaluronic Acid in our skin decreases, leading to dryness, loss of elasticity, and the appearance of fine lines.

Skincare products containing Hyaluronic Acid help replenish moisture, improve skin texture, and maintain a healthy skin barrier without feeling heavy or greasy.

Key Benefits

Deep Hydration

Attracts moisture from the environment and locks it into the skin, providing long-lasting hydration and preventing dryness.

Plumper, Smoother Skin

Increases skin's moisture content, making skin appear fuller, softer, and more radiant while reducing rough texture.

Strengthens the Skin Barrier

Well-hydrated skin has a stronger protective barrier, defending against pollution, irritation, and moisture loss.

Reduces Fine Lines

Hydrates skin deeply, making dehydration fine lines appear visibly smoother and less noticeable.

Gentle for All Skin Types

Non-irritating and lightweight—ideal for dry, oily, combination, normal, and sensitive skin.

Balances Oily Skin

Helps prevent excess oil production by keeping skin appropriately hydrated.

Who Should Use It?

Hyaluronic Acid is recommended for almost everyone, especially people with:

  • Dry or dehydrated skin
  • Sensitive skin
  • Oily skin lacking hydration
  • Combination skin
  • Mature skin with fine lines
  • Skin damaged by weather or environmental stress

How to Apply

  1. Cleanse your face using a gentle cleanser.
  2. Leave your skin slightly damp after cleansing or apply a facial mist.
  3. Apply 2–3 drops of Hyaluronic Acid serum evenly across your face and neck.
  4. Gently pat the serum into your skin instead of rubbing.
  5. Immediately follow with a moisturizer to lock in hydration.
  6. In the morning, finish your routine with sunscreen (SPF 30 or higher).
Pro Tip: Hyaluronic Acid works best when applied to damp skin. Applying it to completely dry skin without sealing it with moisturizer may draw moisture out of the deeper skin layers instead.

Ingredients to Avoid

Unlike many active ingredients, Hyaluronic Acid is compatible with almost everything. There are no major ingredients that must be avoided when using Hyaluronic Acid. However:

  • Avoid applying it to completely dry skin.
  • Always use a moisturizer afterward to prevent moisture loss.
  • If using strong exfoliating acids or retinoids, apply Hyaluronic Acid before or after them to help reduce dryness and irritation.

Possible Side Effects

Hyaluronic Acid is generally considered very safe. Some people may experience:

  • Mild irritation if combined with too many active ingredients simultaneously.
  • Temporary tightness if applied to very dry skin without moisturizer.
  • Rare allergic reactions caused by other ingredients in the product formulation rather than Hyaluronic Acid itself.

If irritation persists, discontinue use and consult a dermatologist.
