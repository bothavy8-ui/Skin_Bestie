Skincare Routine

Understanding your skin type is the crucial first step to achieving healthy, glowing skin.

Personalized Guide

Basic Skincare ♡

Perfect for: Everyone and people who are new to skincare

Morning Step

  • Cleanser
  • Moisturizer
  • Sunscreen

Night Step

  • Cleanser
  • Moisturizer / Cream

Intermediate Skincare ♡

People with concerns like acne, dullness, or dehydration

Morning Step

  • Cleanser
  • Vitamin C or Niacinamide Serum
  • Moisturizer
  • Sunscreen

Night Step

  • Cleanser
  • Serum
  • Moisturizer / Cream

Advanced Skincare ♡

People with concerns like acne, dullness, or dehydration

Morning Step

  • Cleanser
  • Toner
  • Serum
  • Moisturizer
  • Sunscreen

Night Step

  • Double Cleanse
  • Toner
  • Retinol (2–3 nights per week)
  • Serum
  • Night Cream
